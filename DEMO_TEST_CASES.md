# HaluCheck Official Demo Test Cases

These are the recommended manual demonstrations. Record the actual answer, claims, evidence, labels, metrics, and timing from each run; do not substitute expected results for measured results.

| ID | Question | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Why does Earth revolve around the Sun? | Supported scientific explanation |
| D2 | Paris is the capital of Germany. | Contradiction/hallucination handling |
| D3 | What is the capital of France? | General knowledge |
| D4 | Who was the first president of the United States? | Historical evidence |
| D5 | What is 15 multiplied by 8? | Mathematical claim |
| D6 | Who discovered gravity and when was the theory published? | Multi-claim extraction |
| D7 | Apple | Ambiguous input handling |
| D8 | What is the current population of India? | Time-sensitive/limited evidence |
| D9 | What is insulin? | Medical/general health routing |
| D10 | A deliberately unsupported niche statement | Neutral/unverified handling |

For each run capture: provider/model, answer, extracted claims, sources, supported/contradicted/neutral counts, evidence coverage, hallucination rate, support rate, processing time, and timing metadata.
